The four differences, in plain terms

Attention is the bottleneck

Agents don't wait politely in one place. Their questions scatter across terminals, tools, and machines. The approvals inbox is the single queue where all of it lands — sorted, batch-clearable, logged.

Fleets burn money visibly or invisibly

Five agents for a day is a real line item. AgentHelm reads the usage your agents already record and shows burn per agent and per project, today, not at month-end.

Continuity is a document, not a memory

Sessions end; work shouldn't restart. The handoff brief is the artifact that carries a task between agents — task, decisions, files, open items, and the successor's first prompt.

Routing beats watching

Staged for v1.1: assign work from one composer to the right tool, in parallel lanes, each with a budget and a stop condition. The helm stops being a monitor and becomes a control.
