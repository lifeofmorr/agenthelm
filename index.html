Fleets don't break down in the coding.
They break down in your attention.

The agents are fast. The bottleneck is the human switching between terminals, catching stalls late, and hand-carrying context from one session to the next. AgentHelm is built for that side of the fleet.

Prompts you never see

A permission request sits in a background terminal for an hour. The agent isn't slow — it's waiting on you, and nobody told you.

Stalls you notice late

An agent finished — or died — forty minutes ago. You find out when you go looking, not when it happens. Stall detection should be automatic.

Handoffs that lose the thread

A session ends and its context ends with it. The successor starts cold — same task, repeated mistakes, decisions re-litigated.
