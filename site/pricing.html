Straight answers.

Do you resell model access?

No. Every tier is bring-your-own agent accounts. Your Claude Code, Codex, and other subscriptions stay yours; AgentHelm coordinates them and never meters messages between your own agents.

Where does my data live?

On your machine. The console runs locally and reads local session data. Burn figures are computed from your agents' own logs, and estimates are labeled estimates.

What counts as an agent?

A concurrent agent session discovered on the machine — a running Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, OpenCode, or generic-adapter process with a session store AgentHelm can read.
